BIOGRAPHY
(Kyung Hyun) Eric Joo is a Creative Technologist working across real-time media, generative AI, and experimental web projects. His practice explores how emerging tools can act as collaborators in storytelling, image-development, and human-machine interfaces.

He works fluently across a range of creative software and workflows, including ComfyUI for generative image systems, Unreal Engine for real-time environments, TouchDesigner for interactive media, and After Effects for video compositing and motion design. He also designs and develops websites from scratch for creative coding purposes.

Eric is particularly interested in the cultural development of shared digital spaces—how collective memory, identity, and interaction are shaped through virtual platforms, tools, and aesthetics.
